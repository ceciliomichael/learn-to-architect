## 1

Inspect project metadata and structure first: Cargo.toml, workspace members, entry points, dependencies, features, README, tests, and build configuration.

## 2

## 3

## 4

## 5

## 6

## 7

## 8

## 9

A small project whose requirements you already understand, such as the unit converter or task CLI.

## 10

The ability to reason about programming concepts, build mental models from evidence, debug systematically, and learn unfamiliar systems independently.
